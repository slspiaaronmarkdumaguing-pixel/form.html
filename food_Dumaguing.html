Food Tag Line

Basil pesto is a flavorful Italian sauce made from fresh basil, garlic, Parmesan cheese, pine nuts, and olive oil. It has a fresh, fragrant aroma with a rich, creamy, and slightly nutty taste. It is commonly served with pasta, bread, chicken, and vegetables.

FOOD RECIPE

Basil Pesto

This classic basil pesto recipe is nutty and bright. We love it on pasta, pizza, and more! See the post above for my favorite pesto sauce variations.

INGREDIENTS

  • ½ cup toasted pine nuts
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 1 small garlic clove
  • ¼ teaspoon sea salt
  • freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 cups basil leaves
  • ¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for a smoother pesto
  • ¼ cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese, optional

INSTRUCTIONS

What is pesto, and how do I make it?

  1. Wash and dry the basil. Make sure the leaves are as dry as possible.
  2. Toast the pine nuts in a dry pan over medium-low heat for 2–3 minutes, stirring often. Let them cool.
  3. Blend the garlic and pine nuts in a food processor until finely chopped.
  4. Add the basil and pulse several times until roughly chopped.
  5. Add the Parmesan, then slowly drizzle in the olive oil while blending until the pesto reaches your preferred consistency.
  6. Season with salt, pepper, and a little lemon juice if desired.
  7. Taste and adjust add more Parmesan for cheesiness, olive oil for a thinner pesto, or basil for a stronger herb flavor.
How about the pasta.
  1. Cook 250–300g pasta, reserving about ½ cup pasta water. Toss the hot pasta with ½–¾ cup pesto, adding a splash of pasta water until creamy.
  2. Tip: Don't cook the pesto directly over high heat; toss it with the hot pasta off the heat to keep the basil fresh and fragrant.

    3. COOKING TEMPERATURE

    • Low Heat (120–160°C): Used to lightly toast the pine nuts and bring out their nutty flavor without burning them.
    • Medium Heat (160–190°C): Can be used when gently heating pesto with pasta, although direct cooking should be kept minimal.
    • No Cooking Heat: Fresh basil pesto is traditionally not cooked. The basil, garlic, Parmesan, pine nuts, and olive oil are blended together and added to cooked pasta after removing it from the heat.
